---
title: Line-up & Takeoff
description: How to properly issue a line-up and takeoff clearance
---

FlightStrips allow multiple strips to occupy the departure bays at once, but only a single aircraft may have runway clearance to take off at any one time.


## Issue line-up
To line an aircraft up for departure, move its strip into the `RWY DEP` bay. \
You can drag the strip into the bay. This places the aircraft in a "line up and wait" state.

Multiple aircraft may occupy the departure bay concurrently. \
Best practice: set the strip's holding point so other controllers can see the intended release point and spacing.

## Issue takeoff
Give takeoff clearance by clicking the runway cell (runway designator) on the target strip.

Runway clearance uses colour to show state:

- Green — runway cleared and confirmed by the controller.
- Red — multiple aircraft has recived landing clerance, confirm on the scope and visually.
